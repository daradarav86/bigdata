computer.csv is a derived database of the Stack Overflow Annual Developer Survey 2025
(https://survey.stackoverflow.co/, https://github.com/StackExchange/Survey/tree/main/packages/archive/2025).

Original: "Survey data licensed under ODbL 1.0, contents under DbCL 1.0." (Stack Exchange, Inc.)
- ODbL 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
- DbCL 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/

Changes: 14 of 172 columns selected with original names; 2,000 of 49,191 responses
sampled at random (seed 42) and sorted by ResponseId. For classroom practice, 6 Age values were blanked, 5 YearsCode values set to -1, and 5 JobSat values set to 99 (see guides/computer.md).
